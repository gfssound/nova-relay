Nova Relay: Windows desktop app
================================

Nova Relay in its own window, for this computer only. No browser, no Node.js and no internet needed:
everything is inside the "Nova Relay" folder. Phones and iPads aren't used in this version.
Works on 64-bit Windows 10 and 11.

Start
  1. Unzip this whole folder somewhere you keep apps (for example Documents, or C:\Nova Relay).
  2. Open the "Nova Relay" folder and double-click "Nova Relay.exe".
     The first time, Windows may say it protected your PC (the app has no Microsoft signature):
     click "More info", then "Run anyway".
  3. Optional: double-click "Install shortcuts.bat" to add Nova Relay to the Start menu and desktop.

Connect to the Nova
  - Plug the Aria-8 in by USB and close NovaTouch (only one program can use the port).
  - The first time, go to Setup > Connection, pick the Aria's COM port and press Connect
    (38400 baud, odd parity). After that Nova Relay reconnects to that port by itself.
  - Close the window to stop Nova Relay.

Update
  Unzip the new version in place of the old folder (run "Install shortcuts.bat" again if you moved it).
  Settings and capture files are kept in C:\Users\<you>\.nova-remote, shared with the other versions.

Remove
  Delete the folder, and the shortcuts if you made them. Settings stay in C:\Users\<you>\.nova-remote.
